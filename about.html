About Shoaks and Sons Trading & Supply

We are a family-led, black-owned company based in Gauteng. We work as a trusted middleman, connecting contractors, builders, and project managers with quality suppliers and materials at fair, competitive prices.

Our Experience

With years of industry relationships across Gauteng, we understand both the builder's needs and supplier capabilities. Our network includes leading material suppliers, wholesalers and manufacturers.

Our Approach

We listen to your project requirements, connect you with the right suppliers, negotiate fair pricing, and ensure reliable delivery. Our role is to remove complexity and add real value to your procurement.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted trade and supply partner in Gauteng, providing seamless access to quality materials, reliable vendors and responsible procurement support.

Our Mission

To connect clients with the best suppliers, manage procurement efficiently, and deliver a smooth, transparent trading experience that saves time and reduces risk.

Our Values

  • Integrity in pricing, supplier selection and all business dealings
  • Reliability in sourcing and consistent quality materials
  • Transparency in every quote and supply agreement
  • Fairness in partnerships that benefit all parties
